# Pricing - Solar Ireland

All prices are for the Republic of Ireland and reflect the 0% VAT rate on domestic solar. The SEAI Solar PV grant of EUR 1,800 is deducted from your final invoice (Republic of Ireland: owner-occupier homes built before 2021, BER C3 or lower). Every quote is itemised after a free home survey, so final pricing depends on system size, roof complexity, and whether you add battery storage.

## Standard Solar PV (4 kWp)
- For a typical 3-4 bedroom home
- Price: EUR 6,500
- Price after the EUR 1,800 SEAI grant: EUR 4,700

## Solar PV + Battery Storage
- Solar panels with a 5 kWh battery for higher self-consumption
- Price: EUR 11,000
- Price after the EUR 1,800 SEAI grant: EUR 9,200

## Typical range
- Residential solar PV systems: EUR 4,500 to EUR 7,500 before the SEAI grant
- Battery storage (5 kWh, added to a system): approximately EUR 4,000 to EUR 5,000 installed

## What is included
- Free home survey and itemised quote
- Free AI electricity bill analysis
- Full SEAI grant application handled on your behalf
- Installation by RECI-registered electricians, usually in a single day
- ESB Networks grid connection and post-install BER assessment
- 25-year manufacturer warranty on panels; 10-year installation warranty

## Grants and incentives
- SEAI Solar PV grant: EUR 1,800 (Republic of Ireland, 26 counties)
- 0% VAT on domestic solar in Ireland
- Clean Export Guarantee: your electricity supplier pays for the surplus you export to the grid
