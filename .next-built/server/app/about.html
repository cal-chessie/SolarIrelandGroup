Our Story

Tired of the hard sell? So were we.

Solar Ireland was founded in 2023 by Callum McGrath after years of working in the Irish solar industry and seeing the same problems repeat: pushy sales tactics, confusing pricing, and homeowners left in the dark about what they were actually getting.

We believed there had to be a better way. A way where homeowners could get honest, jargon-free advice, transparent pricing, and a quality installation — without the pressure.

Today, we're a growing team of certified installers serving all 32 counties. We've completed over 200 installations and helped our customers save more than €1.1 million on their electricity bills. And we're just getting started.
